TRADITIONAL FILIPINO Food

INGREDIENTS

  • Bangus: 1 large milkfish (about 500g–700g), scaled, gutted, and sliced into serving pieces
  • Water or Rice Washing (Hugas-Bigas): 6 to 8 cups
  • Souring Agent: 1 packet (22g–44g) tamarind mix (sinigang sa sampalok) or 1 cup fresh tamarind extract
  • Tomatoes: 3 medium, quartered
  • Onion: 1 medium, sliced
  • Ginger: 1 thumb-sized, sliced
  • Fish Sauce (Patis): 2 to 3 tablespoons (to taste)
  • Finger Chillies (Siling Pansigang): 2 pieces
  • Radish (Labanos): 1 medium, sliced into rounds
  • Eggplant: 1 medium, sliced diagonally
  • Yardlong Beans (Sitaw): 1 bundle, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • Okra: 5 to 6 pieces
  • Water Spinach (Kangkong): 1 bunch, leaves and tender stems separated

PROCEDURE

  1. Wash and prepare all the ingredients, ensuring the vegetables are sliced to bite-sized pieces.
  2. In a pot, combine the water or rice washing (hugas-bigas), sliced tomatoes, onion, and ginger. Bring to a boil.
  3. Reduce the heat and let the aromatics simmer for about 5 minutes to release their flavors.
  4. Pour in the tamarind extract or tamarind powder mix, stirring well until dissolved.
  5. Add the fish sauce (patis) to taste.
  6. Gently drop in the bangus slices and finger chillies. Cover and simmer for 5 to 7 minutes until the fish is cooked through.
  7. Add the harder vegetables first, such as the radish and yardlong beans (sitaw), and cook for 2 minutes.
  8. Add the eggplant, okra, and water spinach (kangkong) stems, simmering for another 2 minutes.
  9. Toss in the water spinach leaves, turn off the heat, and let the residual heat cook the leaves for about 1 minute.
  10. Transfer to a serving bowl and serve hot with a side of white rice.
